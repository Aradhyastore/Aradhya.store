Aradhya • Purpose With Fragrance

Our Mission

Traditional fragrance, meaningful work and a brand built around dignity.

Aradhya is about fragrance with a purpose — preserving traditional product-making while creating meaningful work opportunities for older women.

The Dhoop Cones products shown on this website are connected with the work of older members of the Aradhya self-help group. The initiative helps provide opportunities for them to work, earn and remain actively involved in the community.

Our aim is to bring their work to more customers across India and, in time, to international markets while presenting the products with the dignity and care they deserve.

Aradhya • Since 2018

About Aradhya

A Lucknow-rooted fragrance initiative growing with tradition, care and purpose.

Established in 2018, Aradhya is a Lucknow-based fragrance initiative focused on fragranced Dhoop Cones and a growing collection of traditional fragrance and pooja-related products.

Aradhya's journey is connected with older women working through a self-help-group initiative. The work creates opportunities to participate, earn and contribute through product-making, while bringing their efforts closer to customers.

Today, the Aradhya offering includes fragranced Dhoop Cones, with Ittar / Attar and Pooja Kit collections being developed as upcoming additions. The website is designed to support product discovery, enquiries, order assistance and customer support as the online catalogue grows.
