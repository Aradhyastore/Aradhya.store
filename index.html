Aradhya • Our Story

About Aradhya

A Lucknow-based fragrance brand rooted in tradition.

Established in 2018, Aradhya is based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, and focuses on fragranced Dhupbatti and traditional fragrance products.

Aradhya is building a growing collection of fragrances while staying connected to the traditions and everyday rituals that make fragrance meaningful in Indian homes.

The brand is also connected with older women through a self-help-group initiative, helping create employment and income opportunities.

Aradhya currently serves customers across India and aims to reach international customers as the brand grows.
